How completed work reaches the right audiences through the right channels
Set Before Distribution Begins
Release Tier
Internal — firm only
Preview — named accounts, feedback
Beta — opted-in, marked not-GA
GA — fully released, supported
Commercial Tier
Not for sale
Early access — with terms
For sale — full commercial
Packaging Type
New feature — expansion selling motion
New SKU — full GTM motion required
Pre-Release Gates — All 4 Required
① Support & Documentation
Docs published · Support team briefed · Escalation path defined
② Internal Enablement
Capability brief · Demo available · Talk track · Pricing guidance
③ Regulatory & Compliance
Internal review · Legal sign-off · Segment restrictions documented
④ Sales-Readiness Gate
Tiered by commercial tier — Early access · New feature · New SKU
All 4 gates confirmed → Release authorized
